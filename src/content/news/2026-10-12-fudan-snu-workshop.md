The 2026 Joint Fudan–Seoul National Workshop on Applied and Computational Mathematics took place at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Fudan University, Shanghai, from October 12 to 15, 2026. The workshop was organized jointly by Fudan University and Seoul National University, with Prof. Youngjoon Hong serving as the organizer on the SNU side alongside faculty members from Fudan.

From our lab, Dohyun Park, Hyeonseok Seo, and Juhoon Kang attended the workshop.
