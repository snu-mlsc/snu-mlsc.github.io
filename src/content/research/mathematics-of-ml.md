Our research applies the methods of mathematical analysis to the foundational
questions of machine learning — convergence, error bounds, and generalization.

Alongside this theoretical program, we develop hybrid architectures that embed
the structures of classical numerical analysis — finite difference, finite
element, and spectral methods — directly into neural network design. Together,
these efforts aim to bridge analytical rigor with the practical power of modern
learning systems.
