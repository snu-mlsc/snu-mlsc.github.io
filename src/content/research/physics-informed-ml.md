Physical laws, expressed as partial differential equations, impose constraints
that any faithful model of nature must respect. We design neural architectures
that encode these laws directly into the learning process, so that predictions
remain both accurate and physically consistent.

Drawing on numerical analysis, functional analysis, and operator theory, we
currently apply this framework to climate forecasting, semiconductor phase
transitions, and fluid dynamics.
